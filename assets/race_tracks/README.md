# race_tracks/

Modular road pieces, curbs, barriers and turns.

**Accepted formats:** `.glb`, `.gltf`, `.fbx`
**Licences:** CC0-1.0 or CC-BY-4.0 only.

## Sourcing note

Realistic, modular race-track kits under a pure CC0 licence are essentially not
available: the freely-licensed kits that exist (e.g. on OpenGameArt and itch.io)
are **low-poly / stylised**, which this project's scope excludes. This folder is
therefore left for you to fill from a clean-licence source, or to extend the
fetcher with a provider that meets the realism bar.

If you add pieces, keep them modular (snap-together straights, turns, chicanes,
curbs, barriers) and record the licence and author in `assets/CREDITS.md`.
